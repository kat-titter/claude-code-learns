## Gotchas

- Punctuation the author leaves out (one author drops em dashes from every tweet): read their style skill first.
- A thesis spread across the thread: distil it into the first line ("the thesis should be very distilled").

## What drives reach (sourced, not vibes)

- **The hook is ~95% of it.** First line < ~10 words, stands alone, opens a
  **curiosity gap** or lands a **contrarian / pattern-interrupt**. If it doesn't
  make sense by itself, it won't travel.
- **Specific numbers beat vague** (~3.4× engagement). "28 hours" > "a long time".
- **Velocity decides it** — the first 30–60 min is the strongest ranking signal,
  and **replies are weighted ~27× likes**. So *write to provoke a reply*: a
  question, a hot take, a "what would you add?".
- **Threads:** hook → 5–7 tight value posts → one clear CTA (≈7 is the sweet spot).
  Each post must earn the next.
- **Concrete personal story > generic advice.** Real detail, real stakes.
- **Hashtags: 0–1.** X reads meaning now, so one well-chosen tag (or none) carries further than a row of them.
- **Links in the first reply**, or at the very end: in-post links can suppress reach. One CTA.

- **Plain field words.** Use the terms your audience already uses (DJs: playlist, transition, drop; scientists: their field's terms), rather than the internal names a project grew; see the field-vocabulary skill.

## Voice

Write in the author's own voice, read from their real posts: register (launch, art drop, chat), case, punctuation,
signature phrases and the conversations they join. Keep a voice profile per author and read it first (their
`<name>-style` skill, `references/social-voice.md`). A post that sounds like its author travels further than a polished one.

## Draft → pressure-test

Write **2–3 hook variants**, keep the one that works alone. Then check:
- would a stranger stop scrolling on line 1?
- is there a number or one concrete detail?
- does it bait a reply?
- one CTA, link last (or in reply)?
- does it sound like the author (their voice profile), or like a launch email?
