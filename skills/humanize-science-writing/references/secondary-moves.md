# Moves from other writing skills and explainers

Secondary sources, worth trying. Moved from SKILL.md §3b, 2026-10-26.

- **Outline in points, then write prose** (the two-stage approach in davila7's scientific-writing skill). Write one
  line per paragraph first, and only then sentences.
- **Name the move each section makes** (K-Dense's scientific-writing skill coaches section by section): the
  abstract states the result, methods let someone redo it, results show and discussion interprets.
- **Join beats with "therefore" and "but", not "and then"** (Nicky Case's talk, in a third-party summary). Causal
  links carry an argument, where a list of events only reports one.
- **Show what made you care first** (same source). For a science post, that is the observation that surprised you.
- **Pay attention to the negative space.** Ask what is missing and what its absence says. In the loop table, every
  element without a running total was found and 17 of 20 with one were blank; that gap became the post's discussion.
